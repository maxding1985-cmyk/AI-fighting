## Behavior / User Impact

- Long-running LAN test sessions now automatically remove stale in-memory rooms after configurable TTLs.
- Closed rooms are retained briefly for clients to observe exit state, then removed to reduce memory buildup.
- Developers can tune cleanup with `ROOM_IDLE_TTL_MS`, `CLOSED_ROOM_TTL_MS`, and `ROOM_CLEANUP_INTERVAL_MS`.
